## 1. Who we are

Verifos is operated by Helium Marketing Corporation (HMC), Philippines.

This policy covers the **live pilot interest form** only. It does **not** cover Dipstify Station, ODO / OwnerDirect, or Verifos features that are not collecting data yet (booking checkout, SERA AI chat, provider subscriptions).

HMC is the PIC for this form.

## 2. What we actually collect (pilot)

- Name or company
- Role — station owner, technician, or supplier
- Need / offer — pumps, gensets, parts
- Origin — Philippines, China, or other (technicians and suppliers)
- Station name, area, geographical coverage, or where you ship to the Philippines
- Technicians — optional trade name, years, core-services detail
- Contact (mobile, WeChat, WhatsApp, and/or email)
- Optional note
- Privacy-acceptance timestamp stored with the row

Government ID and certification files from technicians are sensitive personal information. They are used only for human review so we can match you. They are not shown on the public site and do not by themselves grant a Verified trust badge. We still do not collect payment cards, tank readings, or employee payroll data on this form.

## 3. Why / how we use it

- **Consent** — submit after the privacy checkbox
- **Legitimate interests** — match owners with pump/genset technicians and parts/pump suppliers (PH and China listing for PH stations) for this pilot; prevent spam; support the inbox

HMC operators read submissions and may contact you using the details you gave. We do not sell this list. SERA (or any AI) does not read this form and does not dispatch technicians.

## 4. Who sees it

- HMC operators working this pilot
- Processors: Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting)
- Hosting may be outside the Philippines (typical Supabase region: Singapore) — a cross-border transfer disclosed here

## 5. Retention and rights

Interest rows are kept while the pilot is active and for a reasonable follow-up period, then deleted or anonymized unless you ask sooner or a legal hold applies. Technician ID and certification files follow the same rule.

RA 10173 rights: information, access, correction, erasure, objection, portability, NPC complaint (www.privacy.gov.ph).
